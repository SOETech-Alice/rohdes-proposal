Section 2 · What We Found (verified Oct 17, 2026)

Four gaps — four fixes

Every finding below was pulled from public sources: the live web, the domain registry, the archived site, and your own Facebook page.

Gap 1

A parked website on a domain you own

rohdesleather.com is registered through March 2029 but resolves to a GoDaddy parking page. Customers who search your name find nothing.

The fix

Rebuild the site on the domain you already pay for — the 2022 archived version (services, Fred & Adam's story) gives us a head start.

Gap 2

Every order starts with a phone call

There's no way to request a quote online. After-hours visitors — often at their garage or bike at night — have nowhere to leave details.

The fix

A simple quote-request form that emails the shop directly. No backend, no accounts, no training needed — we show you the starter on the next page of this proposal.

Gap 3

Stale directory listings

Yellowpages links to the dead site; AllPages still lists "Jim Rohde, Owner"; hours shown as "8–3" in one directory vs. your real 10–5 Mon–Fri.

The fix

Citation cleanup: correct name, owner, hours, and links across the major directories so every listing points to the new site.

Gap 4

Facebook-only social presence

Your Facebook page (197 likes, "serving our community since 1979!") is the only active channel — no Instagram, no Google posts, no photos of finished work.

The fix

Photo-forward showcase pages plus a light social routine — before/after shots are your best advertising, and they cost nothing but a photo.
